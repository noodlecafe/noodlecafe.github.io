Privacy

Your email and this site

Short version: I only use your email to send you the cheat sheet and guitar lessons from Noodlecafé. I never sell it or share it with anyone else.

What I collect

When you ask for a cheat sheet, you give me your email address. I also note which cheat sheet you asked for, so the lessons I send match it.

Where it's kept

Emails are kept and sent by Sender, an email service based in the EU. The site counts visits and button clicks without cookies and without storing your IP address, so I can see which cheat sheets help most.

What I send

A few short emails with guitar lessons, and sometimes an invite to Noodlecafé Sessions, the paid membership. Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you're off the list.

Questions or deletion

Email noodle@noodlecafemusic.com and I'll answer, or delete everything I have about you.
